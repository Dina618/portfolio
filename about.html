Curious by nature.
Designed for people.

Product Designer creating thoughtful digital experiences through curiosity, empathy, and research.

Curiosity has always been the thread connecting everything I love.

It started with drawing — as a kid, I loved sketching the characters on tea tins at home, and before long, classmates were asking for one of my own.

That same curiosity led me to study Multiplatform Journalism, where I learned to tell stories, understand people, and communicate ideas clearly.

After graduating, I worked in brand and content design, shaping how products and stories showed up visually — and found myself asking not just how something looked, but how people actually experienced it.

That question is what pulled me toward product design, and later, a Master's in Interactive Design at Northeastern University.

Somewhere along the way, that curiosity found its home in product design.
